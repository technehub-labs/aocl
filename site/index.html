About AOCL

AOCL formalizes the foundational AOCL research — Autonomous Operations and Closed Loops introductory guide (v1.2.0) as a versioned, tool-supported model: the L1–L5 Closed Loop Automation maturity ladder across three assessment vectors, the informative realization heuristic R = √(E² + (C·V_d)²), the Fast/Tactical/Slow loop taxonomy, and a CLAM-aligned loop modeler.

Naming. This toolkit concerns closed-loop automation maturity. It is distinct from the lowercase mama method (measuring and managing autonomy — decision-rights grants) and from the MAMA umbrella organization; see docs/naming.md.

Everything runs locally. No telemetry, no CDN, no accounts. The modeler's state lives in your browser; exports are plain files.
